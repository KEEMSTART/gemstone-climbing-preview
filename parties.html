Parent questions, answered

What's the minimum age?

Kids around 4 and up can climb on our auto-belays — they make it easy and safe for young climbers. Younger siblings are absolutely welcome to watch and cheer from the party room.

Is gear included?

Yes — rental harness and rental shoes are included for every climber in the party. Just have everyone wear socks.

Do guests need to sign anything?

Yes — every guest who enters the climbing areas needs a free online waiver, and kids need a parent or guardian to sign it. Easiest move: send the waiver link along with your invitations so everyone arrives ready to climb.

What should we bring?

Bring your cake, snacks, drinks, plates, and decorations. We provide the party space, tables, and of course the climbing. Keep it simple — we handle the rest.

Can we bring food and cake?

Absolutely. The private party room has plenty of space for food, cake, presents, and all the birthday chaos. Outside food and cake are always welcome.

Can adults climb too?

Of course! Parents and older siblings are welcome to get on the wall right alongside the kids. It's a party for everyone.

How do you keep kids safe?

Every party includes a Gemstone helper and a private belayer who runs the climbing side. Auto-belays keep things simple and safe — no prior experience required.

What's the deposit & booking policy?

A $100 deposit reserves your date, and parties need to be booked at least 5 days in advance. Hosts can arrive 10 minutes early to bring in food and decorations.
