Gemstone Adaptive Climbing Program

everyBODY can climb.

The first fully ADA-accessible climbing gym in the world — and a program built to prove the walls are for everyone.

Our mission

"Empower individuals of all abilities by building self-esteem, confidence and lifetime skills through climbing, yoga and fitness."

🏔️ Launched October 2019 🌍 World's first fully ADA-accessible climbing gym 📺 Featured on Idaho Public Television's Outdoor Idaho

Who the program serves

Climbers of every ability, including people living with:

🧠

Neurological

Autism, cerebral palsy, traumatic brain injury, multiple sclerosis, spina bifida.

👂

Sensory

Hearing and vision impairments — staff and volunteers adapt every climb.

💜

Physical & emotional

PTSD, paralysis, and amputations. All equipment is provided to get everyBODY on the wall.

How it works

1

Reach out to Patty

Program manager Patty McFarling will talk through goals and needs.

2

We provide everything

Adaptive equipment, trained volunteers — medical and climbing professionals.

3

Get on the wall

Build strength, confidence, and lifetime skills — at your pace.
